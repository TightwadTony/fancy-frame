How can we help?

Find a quick answer below or send us a support request. When reporting a problem, include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Contact us at fancy-frames-support.woof028@simplelogin.com. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information. You may also view or report public issues on GitHub.

Troubleshooting

Common questions

The app cannot find my frame

Confirm that your iPhone or iPad and your FancyFrame are connected to the same Wi-Fi network. In iOS Settings, open Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure FancyFrame is enabled. If it still does not appear, restart the frame and reopen the app.

I cannot upload a photo

Make sure the frame is online and reachable from the app. If iOS asks for photo access, allow access to the photos you want to send. You can review this permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → FancyFrame.

My frame is asking for a password

Enter the API password configured on your FancyFrame. This may be different from your Wi-Fi or photo-share password. If you recently changed it, sign in again using the new password.

Changes are not appearing on the frame

Check that the frame still appears online in the app, then try the change again. For photo changes, allow a moment for larger uploads to finish. Restarting the frame can resolve a stalled slideshow.

How do I remove app data?

Signing out removes the saved authentication token for that frame. Deleting the FancyFrame app removes its locally stored preferences and credentials from your iPhone or iPad. Photos stored on the physical frame must be removed from the Photos screen or from the frame's network share.
