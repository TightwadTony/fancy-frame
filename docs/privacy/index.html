Privacy policy

Built to keep things local.

FancyFrame does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. The app communicates directly with compatible photo frames on your local network.

Effective September 7, 2026

01

Information we collect

The developer does not receive or collect personal information through the FancyFrame app. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or a developer-operated cloud service.

02

Photos

The app accesses only the photos you choose for upload. Selected photos may be resized and converted on your device, then sent directly to the FancyFrame you select over your local network. Your photos are not sent to the developer or to a third-party service.

03

Local network access

FancyFrame uses local-network access and Bonjour to find and communicate with compatible frames. During normal use, the app exchanges frame information, settings, photo information, and commands directly with your frame. This communication stays on your local network, except when your frame independently checks GitHub for available software updates.

04

Credentials and preferences

Authentication tokens are stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device. App preferences, such as your selected upload size, are stored locally using iOS storage. Passwords you enter are sent directly to the selected frame on your local network and are not sent to the developer.

05

Retention and deletion

Local app preferences remain until you change them or delete the app. Signing out removes the saved authentication token for that frame. Photos uploaded to your FancyFrame remain on the frame until you delete them through the app or the frame's network share.

06

Third parties and children

The app does not share information with third parties and is not directed to children under 13. Because the developer does not collect personal information through the app, no personal information from children is knowingly collected.

07

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will reflect the latest revision. For privacy questions or requests, use the FancyFrame support page.
